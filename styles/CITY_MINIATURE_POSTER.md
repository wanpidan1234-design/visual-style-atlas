# 高细节城市微缩海报 / City Miniature Poster

## 风格特征

- 画幅：优先 4:5 竖版；顶部留白约 16–20%，主体连续铺开。
- 标题：白底、超大黑色窄体标题；小号地区名与一句副标题；可配简洁天际线剪影。
- 主体：斜俯视微缩城市场景，完整深色方形底座；以地标建立视觉层级，街区、植物、人物和交通提供精密尺度。
- 色彩：明亮自然，砖红、石灰白、绿色植被、蓝绿水面；画面精细且有收藏海报质感。
- 内容：优先突出该主题的核心地标。足球、球队标志、赛季信息不是风格必需元素。
- 地理：允许为构图压缩地理距离，保留主要地标的可辨识结构；如要求真实地图，需另行提供准确空间资料。
- 文字：只保留标题、简短副标题和必要标签，避免微小乱码。

## 可替换参数

`SUBJECT`、`REGION`、`SUBTITLE`、`LANDMARKS`、`NATURAL_FEATURES`、`PALETTE`、`VIEWPOINT`、`ASPECT_RATIO`。

## 通用提示词

Create one premium [ASPECT_RATIO] miniature-panorama poster of [SUBJECT], [REGION]. Use the saved city miniature poster reference for style and layout only. White upper heading area, oversized bold condensed black title "[SUBJECT]", small region line "[REGION]", restrained subtitle "[SUBTITLE]", and a delicate skyline silhouette. Below, show a richly detailed, continuous miniature environment from [VIEWPOINT], resting on a thick clean dark display plinth. Organize [LANDMARKS] into a clear visual hierarchy, preserving each landmark's recognizable architecture. Add [NATURAL_FEATURES], characteristic streets, trees, tiny people and appropriate transportation. Precise small-scale craft, refined vivid [PALETTE], crisp natural light, convincing materials, beautiful atmospheric depth. Artistic compressed geography is allowed. The complete front base edge remains visible. Keep the main subject visually dominant. Use no unrelated sports branding, no season text, no parchment, no architectural sketch insets, no wooden plaque and no watermark. Only the requested title and minimal meaningful labels.
