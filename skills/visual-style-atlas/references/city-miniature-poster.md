# 高细节城市微缩海报

默认 4:5 竖版，顶部白底约 16–20%，超大黑色窄体标题、小地区名与简短副标题、细小天际线。下方连续立体城市全景置于完整深色方形底座上；地标有主次，精密街区、人车和植物建立尺度。色彩明亮自然，水景仅在主题适合时出现。

替换参数：`SUBJECT`、`REGION`、`SUBTITLE`、`LANDMARKS`、`NATURAL_FEATURES`、`PALETTE`。

```text
Create one premium vertical 4:5 miniature-panorama poster of [SUBJECT], [REGION]. Use the supplied image for style and layout only. White heading area with oversized condensed black title "[SUBJECT]", small region line "[REGION]", restrained subtitle "[SUBTITLE]" and a delicate skyline silhouette. Below, show a richly detailed continuous miniature environment from an elevated oblique viewpoint on a thick clean dark display plinth. Organize [LANDMARKS] into a clear hierarchy, preserving recognizable structures. Include appropriate [NATURAL_FEATURES], streets, trees, tiny people and transportation. Refined vivid [PALETTE], crisp sunlight and convincing material detail. Artistic compressed geography is allowed. Keep the complete front plinth edge visible. No unrelated sports branding, season text, parchment, sketch-study insets, wooden plaque or watermark. Only requested titles and minimal meaningful labels.
```

用户要城市全景时以城市地标为主，不因原参考含有球队元素而自动生成足球海报。其他主题同理，以指定内容替换标题和场景。
