# 复古建筑手绘研究图

默认方形。米黄旧纸、轻微纤维与斑驳、钢笔与铅笔排线、淡水彩。主透视图居中，四周是立面、构件局部、结构剖面和小型环境图。棕灰、浅赭、灰蓝为主，主题色轻点缀。左上手写标题；副标题衬线字；不画现代 UI 卡片边框。

替换参数：`SUBJECT`、`LOCATION`、`ARCHITECTURAL_FEATURES`、`DETAIL_STUDIES`、`TITLE`、`LABELS`。

```text
Create one square vintage architectural study sheet of [SUBJECT] in [LOCATION], using the supplied image only as a style and composition reference. Warm aged ivory parchment, delicate graphite construction lines, fine sepia ink, crosshatching and restrained translucent watercolor in warm gray, ochre and dusty blue. A large three-quarter perspective view shows [ARCHITECTURAL_FEATURES], with surroundings softly fading into paper. Around the margins arrange a small elevation, meaningful [DETAIL_STUDIES], a simple structural section and a contextual site sketch. Subtle drafting lines and compass rose. Handwritten upper-left title "[TITLE]", location "[LOCATION]", serif subtitle "ARCHITECTURAL STUDY". Use only short accurate [LABELS]. No photographic texture, no 3D diorama, no wooden plaque, no unrelated bridge elements, no invented dimensions, no watermark.
```

旧画风不代表把现代建筑改成古建筑。细节图按主体选择，不照抄塔桥窗户与桥塔。若需要工程准确性，先取得相应资料。
