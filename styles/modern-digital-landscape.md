# 现代数字风景插画

风格 ID：`modern-digital-landscape`；选项编号：5。

## 视觉规则

- 现代数字绘画与编辑插画质感：清晰轮廓、凝练色块、受控渐变、选择性的精细描绘。
- 用主景、次景和远景建立层次；主体醒目，天空或雾气保留呼吸空间。
- 配色围绕题材选择主色与冷暖对比，示例的金黄银杏不是其他题材的固定元素。
- 光影通透，局部边缘透光，投影柔和；建筑和自然结构保持可信。
- 默认无文字、满版构图，不加旧纸、做旧边框、手绘注记或模型底座。
- 画幅随用途选择；单树与纵深山景可用 4:5 竖版。需要标题时另行确定文字与留白。
- 风格现代化只改变表现媒介，不把古寺改成现代建筑，不随意增加地标或人物。

## 可复用提示词

```text
Create a contemporary editorial landscape illustration of [SUBJECT] in [LOCATION / SETTING], [SEASON / TIME]. Use [ASPECT RATIO] composition. Modern refined digital painting, clean silhouettes, confident layered shapes, elegant controlled gradients, selective detailed accents and luminous natural light. Build a clear hierarchy around [MAIN SUBJECT], with [SUPPORTING ELEMENTS] and atmospheric depth through [BACKGROUND]. Use [PALETTE] with deliberate warm-cool contrast, soft shadows and restrained negative space. Preserve credible natural forms and culturally appropriate architecture. Full-bleed artwork, no text unless explicitly specified, no watermark, no aged paper, no distressed borders, no retro collage, no miniature display base. The scene is an artistic interpretation, not documentary evidence.
```

转绘已有成图时，先明确输入为编辑目标并列出保留项；上述提示词负责新的表现形式。
